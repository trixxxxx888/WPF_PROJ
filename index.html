What is Artificial Intelligence (A.I.)?

Welcome to your beginners guide to Artificial Intelligence(AI). We are here to guide you through 4 key areas:

  • Key Concepts & Background of A.I.
  • Applications of A.I.
  • Positive Impacts of A.I.
  • Negative Impacts of A.I.

The foundation of Concepts and Background about Artificial Intelligence(A.I.).

  • Definition of AI : Artificial intelligence represents computers and machines being engineered to replicate human cognitive functions such as learning, reasoning, and problem-solving. So that they could execute the task that is given to them.
  • Main Technologies : It relies on key areas like machine learning, neural networks, natural language processing, and robotics.

    • What are these technologies?

    • Machine Learning : Its a method where the computer learns from its old data and finds patterns to get smarter on its own over time. So that they can make better decisions in the near future.
    • Neural Networks : These are computing systems inspired by the human brain, They are designed to recognize hidden or unsual patterns and make decisions based on data that they are given.
    • Natural Language Processing : This technology lets the computer understand, interpret, and talk back in human languages. Powering chatbots, smart assistants like Siri, Alexa, Google home and also does voice translations.
    • Robotics : This field combines engineering and A.I. to build machines that can sense their surroundings and perform tasks in the real world just like the HaiDiLao robots you see.
  • Content Creation AI : Its basically a specialized area of A.I. focused on creating entirely new content like texts, images, music, audio and code not just analyzing and or organising existing "data"(content).
  • Real World Examples : Just like you opening your phone in the morning, A.I. is already implimented as your phone uses facial recognition to unlock it by using instant visual scans. Reccomending you videos on youtube based on your viewing history so on and so forth...
  • Independent Systems & Creative Tools : This are machines and software that can operate with out any human supervision like self driving cars.
  • Risks & Challenges : While beneficial, the rapid growth of AI brings challenges such as mirror biases from training data, Gathering massive datasets raises privacy concerns, and potential job displacement.
